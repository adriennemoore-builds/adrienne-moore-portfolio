## Case Studies

**Institutional Market GTM — Common App, 2024–Present**
Built the full sales enablement ecosystem (personas, segmentation, lead scoring, battlecards, playbooks) for a lean sales team closing multi-stakeholder enterprise deals. Authored the FY27 GTM Strategy adopted by leadership as the operating plan for market expansion. [Read the full case study →](./docs/Adrienne_Moore_Portfolio_26.pdf)

**Net-New Market Entry — Community Colleges, Common App**
Architected Common App's first-ever entry into the community college sector — 39% of U.S. undergraduates — with no existing playbook, no research budget, and no organizational precedent. Built personas from primary research with college presidents and VPs, launched conference strategy, and secured the first cohort of community college members.

**Technical Product, Skeptical Buyer — MeCision (Mouse & Matter)**
Built end-to-end GTM infrastructure for a mental health technology platform where the product was clinically sophisticated and the buyer was stigma-aware and slow to trust. Diagnosed trust as the core conversion barrier before writing a word of messaging.

**AI Product Design & Automation — Saunders Suite (Mouse & Matter)**
Designed and built a personal AI operating system for a founder with ADHD — five integrated apps and one automated morning briefing. Same diagnostic method as the GTM work, applied to a different kind of build. (Live product is a private client deliverable — happy to walk through it directly on request.)
